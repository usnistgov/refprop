Introduction#

Refprop is a software package developed by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) that provides comprehensive thermodynamic and transport property data for a variety of industrially important pure fluids and mixtures. Refprop is commonly utilized in the fields of engineering, thermodynamics, and process simulation. The current version of Refprop, Version 11, can be accessed here: https://www.nist.gov/srd/refprop .
